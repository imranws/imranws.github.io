Project Overview

The goal was to build a fast, lightweight eCommerce website without a page builder or unnecessary frontend code. The storefront is fully custom-built and connected to WordPress as the backend, giving the site the flexibility of WordPress with a clean and focused frontend experience.

The shopping experience was designed with Bangladeshi customers in mind, with clear product browsing, an easy checkout flow, mobile-friendly interactions, and trust-building elements throughout the journey. The WordPress backend also keeps product, order, analytics, traffic, and customer-behaviour management in one place.
